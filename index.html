About

I am a PhD candidate in Accounting at the Antai College of Economics & Management, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research examines how information, institutions, and technological change shape corporate decisions, financial reporting, and governance outcomes.

My recent research focuses on social media, artificial intelligence, biodiversity, and entrepreneurship.

赵禺，上海交通大学安泰经济与管理学院会计学博士研究生，研究方向包括公司金融、财务会计与公司治理。他主要关注信息环境、制度变迁与技术发展如何影响企业决策、财务报告和治理结果。
